DSCR Loan Terms & Program Details

What Is DSCR?

DSCR stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio. In simple terms: DSCR = Monthly Rent ÷ Monthly Mortgage Payment (PITI + HOA).

A DSCR of 1.0 means rent equals the payment. Above 1.0 means the property generates positive cash flow. Most programs begin around 1.0–1.1, with options for stronger or weaker DSCR depending on the deal.

We evaluate the property's fair market rent, the estimated payment, and your credit profile. We do not ask for personal tax returns, W-2s, or pay stubs.

Common Questions

DSCR Loan FAQs

Do I really not need to provide income documentation?
In most DSCR programs, no personal tax returns, W-2s, or pay stubs are required. The property's rental income and value drive the approval. Some programs may require minimal documentation for credit or reserves verification.
What DSCR do I need to qualify?
Most programs begin around a DSCR of 1.0 to 1.1. There are options for scenarios below 1.0, though pricing adjusts accordingly. We'll estimate your DSCR and match you to the right program based on your property's numbers.
Can I use DSCR loans for short-term rentals like Airbnb?
Yes, many DSCR programs accommodate short-term rentals where eligible. Lenders typically use a market rent schedule or a percentage of actual STR income to qualify the property. We'll review your specific situation.
How many DSCR loans can I have at once?
Unlike conventional financing, most DSCR programs do not have strict limits on the number of financed properties. This makes them ideal for investors with growing portfolios who want to keep scaling without hitting conventional limits.
