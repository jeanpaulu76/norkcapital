Common Questions

Commercial Loan FAQs

When does a property become "commercial"?
Properties with 5 or more residential units, any retail, office, industrial, or mixed-use property fall under commercial lending guidelines. Once you move beyond 4 units, residential lenders step aside and commercial lenders take over.
What is the typical down payment on commercial properties?
Most commercial programs require 20–35% down depending on property type, borrower experience, and loan program. Some value-add deals with strong upside may have different structures. We will outline options specific to your deal.
How long does commercial financing take?
Commercial loans typically take 30–60 days from application to close, though bridge-style commercial products can move faster. Timeline depends on property complexity, appraisal, and environmental requirements.
Do I need to personally guarantee the loan?
Personal guarantees are common in commercial lending but not always required. Non-recourse options exist for certain property types and loan structures. We will identify the right program for your situation and risk tolerance.
