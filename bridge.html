When It Makes Sense

5 Situations Where a Bridge Loan Wins

Bridge loans give you speed and flexibility that traditional lenders simply cannot match.

🏃
Acquisition Speed

Found an off-market deal or auction property requiring a fast close? A bridge loan funds in days — not months — so you don't lose the opportunity to a cash buyer or faster competitor.

⏰
Loan Maturity Bridge

Your existing loan is coming due and your permanent refinance isn't ready. A bridge loan covers the gap and prevents default or forced sale while you finalize long-term financing.

🏗️
Stabilization Period

Purchased a property that needs light rehab or lease-up before it qualifies for a DSCR loan? A bridge loan gives you 12–24 months to stabilize the asset before refinancing.

🔄
Buy Before You Sell

Purchase your next investment property before selling the current one. Close on the new property now and pay off the bridge when the old property sells at full market value.

💼
Value-Add Projects

Light renovation, unit conversion, or repositioning that increases value. Bridge loans are ideal for cosmetic updates or lease-up strategies where you plan to hold long-term.

📋
Complex Deals

Deals that don't fit conventional boxes — partnership buyouts, estate properties, unusual property types — bridge loans can often accommodate what traditional lenders won't touch.

Common Questions

Bridge Loan FAQs

How fast can a bridge loan close?
Many bridge programs close in 7–14 business days. Expedited closings are available for time-sensitive deals. We work with lenders who understand that speed is the product for real estate investors.
What is the exit strategy requirement?
Bridge loans require a clearly defined exit — either selling the property or refinancing into permanent financing such as a DSCR loan. The strength of your exit strategy is a key part of underwriting approval.
Do I need good credit for a bridge loan?
Credit matters but bridge loans are more flexible than conventional financing. The property value, deal strength, and exit strategy carry significant weight in the approval process alongside your credit profile.
What property types qualify for bridge loans?
Single-family, multifamily (2–36+ units), mixed-use, and many commercial properties. Whether you are acquiring a duplex or a small apartment building, bridge programs can often accommodate the deal.
